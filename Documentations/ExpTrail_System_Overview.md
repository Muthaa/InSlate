Author: Philip Kyalo

1. Executive Summary

InSlate is an AI-powered personal finance platform designed to help individuals understand, manage, and improve their financial behavior through automated transaction capture, intelligent analytics, predictive insights, and personalized recommendations.

Unlike traditional expense trackers that focus primarily on recording expenses, PesaScope functions as a Financial Operating System by integrating transaction ingestion, financial analytics, budgeting, behavioral insights, AI-powered financial assistance, and long-term wealth tracking into a single platform.

The platform is designed with a mobile-first architecture and is initially optimized for the Kenyan financial ecosystem, particularly M-Pesa, while maintaining an extensible architecture capable of supporting banks, digital wallets, investments, and additional payment providers.

1. Vision Statement

1. Mission Statement

Empower individuals to make better financial decisions through automation, analytics, behavioral insights, and artificial intelligence.

1. Problem Statement

Current finance applications primarily answer:

What did I spend?

Users actually need answers to more meaningful questions:

Why am I overspending?
What habits are affecting my finances?
Where can I reduce unnecessary expenses?
How healthy are my finances?
What will happen if I continue spending at this rate?
Can I achieve my savings goals?
Most existing platforms provide descriptive analytics but lack predictive and behavioral intelligence.

1. Product Philosophy

The platform follows five principles:

Automation First

Minimize manual data entry by automatically capturing financial transactions through SMS parsing, statement imports, and future banking integrations.

Intelligence Over Reporting

The objective is not to produce charts but meaningful financial insights.

Privacy by Design

Sensitive financial information remains encrypted, with user control over cloud synchronization and data sharing.

Offline First

Core functionality should remain available without internet connectivity.

Explainable AI

Every recommendation generated by AI should be transparent, traceable, and understandable by the user.
